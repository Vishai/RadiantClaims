## Overview

RadiantClaims provides turnkey RECA claim packet preparation services to qualified attorney firms. We handle document collection, records requests, client coordination, and packet assembly. You provide legal review, advice, and representation.

---

## The Product: Completed RECA Claim Packet

Each packet includes:
- ✅ Verified eligibility screening (residence + cancer + latency)
- ✅ Collected residence documentation (school, church, employer, utility, etc.)
- ✅ Medical records obtained (pathology, operative reports, cancer registry)
- ✅ Survivor documentation if applicable (birth/marriage/death certificates)
- ✅ All signed authorizations and releases
- ✅ Pre-filled DOJ RECA claim forms
- ✅ Quality checklist with PacketScore ≥80

**Your work:** Review packet, provide legal advice, sign forms, file with DOJ. Estimated 2-3 hours attorney time per case.

## Pricing & Payment

### Standard Rate
**$1,000 per packet** (adjusted for DIY upgrades, see below)

### Payment Timing
**60 days after DOJ award approval**
- Aligns payment with when you receive funds from government
- Government typically pays 30-45 days after approval
- You provide proof of award notification; we invoice; payment due 60 days from award date

### Adjusted Pricing for DIY Upgrades
Some clients start on our DIY platform ($149.99 setup + $24.99/month) and later request legal representation. In these cases:
- **You pay:** $1,000 minus what client already paid us
- **Example:** Client paid us $225 via DIY → You pay us $775
- **Client pays you:** Full $2,000 (same as any client)
- **Your margin:** Higher profit on these cases

### Quality Acceptance Period
- 10 business days from packet delivery to reject for quality issues
- After acceptance, packet is your responsibility
- Can't reject later due to quality concerns

---

## Denied Claims

If a claim is denied, payment depends on reason:

| Denial Reason | Payment Due |
|---------------|-------------|
| Packet quality defects RadiantClaims should have caught | $0 |
| Eligibility issues (latency, cancer type, residence insufficient) | $500 partial |
| DOJ error, procedural issues, or circumstances beyond control | $1,000 full |

Attorney provides written explanation of denial reason within 30 days.
