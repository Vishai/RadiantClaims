## Key Terms Summary

### The Deal
- **You sell:** Completed RECA claim packets (PacketScore ≥80)
- **Attorney buys:** $1,000 per packet (minus DIY credits)
- **Attorney sells to client:** $2,000 for legal representation
- **Attorney profit:** $1,000 per case (excellent margins)

### Payment Timing (Critical)
- **Trigger:** 60 days after DOJ award approval
- **Why:** Attorney has cash in hand from government payment
- **Risk sharing:** Both parties want approvals, not just filings

### DIY Upgrade Economics
- **Client paid you:** $149.99 setup + ($24.99 × months)
- **You bill attorney:** $1,000 - (client payments)
- **Example:** Client paid $225 → Attorney pays you $775
- **Attorney charges client:** $2,000 total (same as any client)
- **Monthly billing stops** when client upgrades to legal

### Quality Protection
- **10-day acceptance window:** Attorney must reject within 10 days or packet is accepted
- **No late rejections:** Can't claim quality issues after acceptance period
- **Remediation rights:** You get 10 days to fix identified deficiencies

### Denied Claims
- **Packet quality defects:** $0 payment (your fault)
- **Eligibility issues:** $500 partial (judgment call)
- **External/procedural:** $1,000 full (nobody's fault)

## Important Notes

### Not Fee-Splitting
This is **NOT** a fee-splitting or co-counsel arrangement. You're selling a product (completed packets) to attorneys. They're your customer, not your partner. This structure avoids all the ethical issues around non-lawyers sharing legal fees.

### State Bar Considerations
Section 13.1 is intentionally blank for state-specific requirements. Each attorney should:
- Review with their state bar ethics rules
- Add any required disclosures or provisions
- Confirm compliance with their jurisdiction

### Customization Points
Before sending to attorneys, customize:
- Section 3.6: Volume discounts (optional - remove if not offering)
- Section 9.1: Minimum commitments (choose Option A or B)
- Section 9.2: Maximum capacity (insert initial number)
- Section 12.4: Contact information
- Section 12.5: Governing law and venue
- Section 13: State-specific and additional terms
