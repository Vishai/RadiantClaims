RadiantClaims provides a self-service digital platform designed to help users organize and prepare documentation for Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (RECA) claims. The Platform includes:

- Document organization and tracking tools
- AI-powered document review and feedback
- AI coaching and guidance through the RECA application process
- Self-service DOJ RECA claim form preparation
- Records request templates and tracking
- E-signature coordination for authorization forms
- Educational resources and guides
