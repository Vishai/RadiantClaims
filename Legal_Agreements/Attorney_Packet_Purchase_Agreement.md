## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, RadiantClaims provides document preparation and case management services for Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (RECA) claims;

## 1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES

### 1.1 Product Definition
RadiantClaims shall prepare and deliver to Attorney completed RECA claim packets ("Packets") meeting the quality standards defined in Section 2 below.

### 1.2 Packet Components
Each Packet shall include, at minimum:
- (a) Completed eligibility screening with documented residence history, cancer diagnosis, and latency period verification
- (b) Collected residence documentation including but not limited to school records, church records, employment records, utility bills, voter registration, or other acceptable proof
- (c) Medical documentation including pathology reports, operative reports, cancer registry confirmations, or other diagnostic proof
- (d) Survivor documentation if applicable, including birth certificates, marriage certificates, death certificates, and relationship verification
- (e) Signed authorization forms including HIPAA medical releases, residence record releases, and limited power of attorney for records requests
- (f) Pre-filled Department of Justice RECA claim forms ready for attorney review and signature
- (g) Quality checklist documenting completeness and PacketScore (defined below)

### 1.3 Non-Legal Services
RadiantClaims provides document preparation, collection, and organizational services only. RadiantClaims does not:
- (a) Provide legal advice or legal representation
- (b) Create attorney-client relationships with claimants
- (c) Practice law or engage in activities requiring a law license
- (d) Make legal determinations regarding claim eligibility or merit

All legal services, advice, and representation are the sole responsibility of Attorney.

## 2. QUALITY STANDARDS

### 2.1 PacketScore Methodology
Each Packet shall achieve a minimum PacketScore of 80 points based on the following criteria:
- Residence documentation (maximum 60 points): +20 points per category of acceptable proof, up to 3 categories
- Primary cancer proof verified (25 points): Pathology report, operative report, or cancer registry confirmation
- Latency period satisfied (10 points): Documented time between exposure and diagnosis meets RECA requirements
- Identity documentation complete (10 points): Birth certificate, government ID, Social Security verification
- Survivor documentation complete if applicable (10 points): All relationship and identity documents for eligible survivors
- All releases and authorizations signed (5 points): Complete execution of necessary consent forms

### 2.2 Quality Acceptance Period
Attorney shall have ten (10) business days from receipt of a Packet to inspect and either:
- (a) Accept the Packet as meeting quality standards, or
- (b) Reject the Packet in writing with specific deficiencies identified

Failure to reject within ten (10) business days constitutes acceptance.

### 2.3 Remediation of Deficiencies
If Attorney properly rejects a Packet within the acceptance period:
- (a) RadiantClaims shall have ten (10) business days to remedy identified deficiencies
- (b) Upon resubmission, Attorney shall have five (5) business days to review
- (c) If deficiencies cannot be remedied, Attorney may request:
  - (i) Reassignment of the Packet to RadiantClaims for completion by alternative means, or
  - (ii) Cancellation with no payment obligation

### 2.4 Post-Acceptance Quality
Once a Packet is accepted (explicitly or by expiration of rejection period), Attorney may not later claim quality deficiencies as grounds for non-payment or reduced payment, except as provided in Section 4.4 regarding denied claims.

### 3.1 Standard Packet Price
The purchase price for each completed Packet is One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00) ("Standard Rate"), subject to adjustments in Section 3.2.

### 3.2 Adjusted Pricing for DIY Pathway Clients
When a claimant initially engages RadiantClaims through the DIY self-service platform and subsequently requests legal representation:
- (a) The Packet purchase price shall be the Standard Rate minus any fees previously paid by the claimant to RadiantClaims for DIY services
- (b) RadiantClaims shall provide Attorney with documentation of amounts paid by claimant

### 3.5 Invoice and Payment Process

## 4. DENIED CLAIMS AND PAYMENT ADJUSTMENTS

### 4.1 Notification of Denial
If the DOJ denies a claim for which Attorney has accepted a Packet, Attorney shall notify RadiantClaims in writing within thirty (30) days of receiving the denial decision.

### 4.2 Denial Documentation
Attorney's notice shall include:
- (a) Copy of the DOJ denial letter or decision
- (b) Attorney's written analysis of the basis for denial
- (c) Attorney's assessment of whether the denial relates to Packet quality, eligibility issues, or other factors

### 4.3 Payment Based on Denial Category
Payment obligations for denied claims shall be determined as follows:

**(a) Packet Quality Defects ($0 payment due)**  
If the denial resulted from defects or omissions in documentation that RadiantClaims should reasonably have identified and remedied prior to delivery, and such defects were not identified during the quality acceptance period, no payment is due. Examples include:
- Missing required residence documentation that should have been collected
- Insufficient medical proof of covered cancer diagnosis
- Missing survivor relationship documentation
- Mathematical or clerical errors in forms

**(b) Eligibility Issues ($500 partial payment due)**  
If the denial resulted from substantive eligibility determinations that could not be definitively resolved through additional documentation, partial payment of $500 is due. Examples include:
- Insufficient cumulative residence time in downwinder counties
- Cancer type not clearly matching RECA covered categories
- Latency period calculations disputed by DOJ
- Survivor relationship eligibility determinations

**(c) External or Procedural Issues ($1,000 full payment due)**  
If the denial resulted from factors outside both parties' reasonable control or DOJ procedural determinations, full payment is due. Examples include:
- DOJ fund depletion or administrative closure
- Statute of limitations or filing deadline issues
- DOJ policy changes affecting claim evaluation
- Administrative errors by DOJ
- Government shutdown or processing delays

### 4.4 Dispute Resolution for Denial Payment
If the parties disagree on the appropriate payment category for a denied claim:
- (a) Both parties shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days
- (b) If no resolution is reached, either party may demand mediation
- (c) Mediation shall be conducted by a neutral mediator agreed upon by both parties
- (d) Costs of mediation shall be split equally
- (e) If mediation fails, either party may pursue legal remedies

### 4.5 Appeal Rights Preserved
Nothing in this Section 4 shall prevent Attorney from appealing a DOJ denial or pursuing other legal remedies on behalf of the claimant. Payment obligations under this Agreement are independent of appeal outcomes.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 11.1 Initial Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of one (1) year.

### 11.2 Renewal
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

### 11.3 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.

### 11.4 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if:
- (a) The other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of written notice
- (b) The other party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases business operations
- (c) Attorney's law license is suspended, revoked, or Attorney is otherwise prohibited from practicing law
- (d) RadiantClaims engages in unauthorized practice of law

### 11.5 Effect of Termination - Active Packets
Upon termination for any reason:
- (a) **Accepted Packets:** All Packets accepted by Attorney prior to termination shall continue through to completion and payment per original terms
- (b) **Pending Packets:** Packets delivered but not yet accepted/rejected shall be returned to RadiantClaims with no payment obligation
- (c) **In-Progress Work:** RadiantClaims shall not deliver new Packets after termination date
- (d) **Payment Obligations:** All unpaid invoices for accepted Packets remain due per original payment terms

### 11.6 Post-Termination Obligations
After termination:
- (a) Attorney shall continue to represent clients from Packets accepted prior to termination
- (b) Platform access shall continue for Attorney's accepted cases until all such cases are resolved
- (c) Confidentiality and non-circumvention obligations survive termination
- (d) Attorney may export case data for Attorney's accepted cases

### 11.7 Transition Assistance
Upon termination, both parties shall cooperate in good faith to ensure smooth transition of active cases and client communications.
