### Step 2: Manually Compile County Vital Records (1 hour)

Use these authoritative sources to quickly build your county contact list:

#### **Utah Counties** (29 counties)
1. Visit: https://vitalrecords.utah.gov/contact
   - State Office: (801) 538-6105
   - [email protected]

2. For county-specific offices, visit:
   - https://www.citydirectory.us/vital-records-utah.html
   - Click each county for contact details

**Priority Utah Counties (Downwinders):**
- Washington County (St. George area)
- Iron County (Cedar City)
- Kane County
- Garfield County
- Beaver County
- Millard County

#### **Nevada Counties** (17 counties)
1. Visit: https://www.citydirectory.us/vital-records-nevada.html

**Priority Nevada Counties:**
- Clark County (Las Vegas) - Largest population
- Nye County - Contains Nevada Test Site
- Lincoln County - Downwind area
- Esmeralda County
- Eureka County
- White Pine County

**Clark County Vital Records:**
- Phone: (702) 759-1010
- Address: 500 S Grand Central Pkwy, Las Vegas, NV 89155

#### **Arizona Counties** (15 counties)
Visit: https://www.citydirectory.us/vital-records-arizona.html

**Priority Arizona Counties:**

#### **New Mexico Counties** (33 counties)
