## County-Specific Notes

### Washington County, UT (St. George)
- **Highest priority** for RECA claims
- Major fallout events: "Dirty Harry" (May 1953), others
- Resources: Hospital, schools, LDS wards
- Modern population: ~180,000 (rapid growth)

### Nye County, NV
- **Contains Nevada Test Site** (ground zero)
- Sparse population, vast area
- Key towns: Tonopah, Beatty, Pahrump
- Mercury (Test Site townsite) records critical

### Clark County, NV (Las Vegas)
- **Largest population** in RECA area
- Vegas population ~64,000 in 1950, ~127,000 in 1960
- Atmospheric testing visible from Strip
- Resources: Major hospitals, extensive records

## Historical Context

**RECA Eligibility Timeline:**
- **1951-1958:** Atmospheric testing peaks
- **1962:** Last atmospheric test (July 1962)
- **Latency requirement:** Cancer diagnosis ≥5 years after exposure (≥2 for leukemia)
- **First eligible diagnosis:** 1956+ (leukemia), 1963+ (solid tumors)

**Why these counties:**
- Prevailing winds carried fallout NE from Nevada Test Site
- Utah "Downwinders" received highest civilian exposure
- Some Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico counties also affected
- Idaho received lighter but documented fallout

## Research Tips

1. **Start with county seats** - Most records centralized there
2. **LDS Family History Library** - Invaluable for UT/ID
3. **Consolidated school districts** - Many 1950s schools closed/merged
4. **Historical societies** - Know where defunct institution records went
5. **State archives** - Hold old county records
