# Chrome Web Store Listing — Radiant Claims Filing Assistant

## Short Description (≤132 characters)

Transfer your prepared RECA claim data from RadiantClaims.com to the DOJ portal with field-by-field guidance and document assistance.

## Detailed Description

**File your RECA claim with confidence — guided every step of the way.**

Radiant Claims Filing Assistant bridges the gap between preparing your Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (RECA) claim on RadiantClaims.com and filing it through the official Department of Justice portal at reca.justice.gov.

### How It Works

1. **Prepare your claim** on RadiantClaims.com — organize your information, gather documents, and verify eligibility.
2. **Install this extension** and navigate to the DOJ filing portal.
3. **Follow field-by-field guidance** — the assistant highlights each form field and shows you the corresponding data from your prepared claim.
4. **Upload supporting documents** with on-screen assistance showing which documents go where.
5. **You submit directly to the DOJ** — your claim goes straight to the government, not through any third party.

### Key Features

- **Field-by-field transfer guidance** — see exactly what to enter in each DOJ portal field
- **Document upload assistance** — clear guidance on which supporting documents to attach and where
- **Secure & private** — your claim data stays between your browser and the DOJ portal; nothing is sent to third parties
- **Local storage only** — cached data is stored in your browser and can be cleared at any time
- **Works with your RadiantClaims.com account** — securely retrieves your prepared claim data via authenticated API

### Important

This extension **assists you in filing your own claim** through the official DOJ portal. RadiantClaims.com helps you prepare; you file directly with the Department of Justice. We do not file claims on your behalf, and we are not affiliated with the DOJ.

### Who Is This For?

Anyone who has prepared a RECA claim on RadiantClaims.com and is ready to submit it through the DOJ's online filing system. The extension eliminates the tedious process of manually copying information between your prepared claim and the government form.
